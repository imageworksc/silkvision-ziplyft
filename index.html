What is Ziplyft?

How is it different from traditional eyelid surgery?

Ziplyft is a minimally invasive upper eyelid lift that physically removes the excess skin causing heavy, hooded or tired-looking eyes.

Unlike traditional upper blepharoplasty, Ziplyft is performed in the physician’s office in less than 15 minutes using local anesthetic, without sutures or cautery — and most patients are typically event-ready within 7–10 days.

It is designed primarily for patients whose main concern is excess upper-eyelid skin.

When your eyes look more tired than you feel

Excess upper-eyelid skin can make your eyes appear heavier, hooded or tired — even when you feel completely rested.

Creams and surface treatments may improve the appearance of the skin, but they do not remove excess skin. Traditional upper blepharoplasty does. Ziplyft offers another option: actual removal of excess upper-eyelid skin through a minimally invasive, physician-performed treatment.

How does Ziplyft work?

Ziplyft uses Compression Skin Contouring — CoCo™ technology — to compress precisely marked excess skin before it is removed.

Compression controls blood flow during treatment, so the targeted skin can be removed without cautery. The edges are then sealed with medical-grade tissue adhesive rather than stitches.

  1. Compress

    Precisely marked excess skin is gently compressed to control blood flow before removal.

  2. Remove

    The targeted skin is removed while the tissue stays compressed, minimizing bleeding without cautery.

  3. Seal

    The skin edges are closed with medical-grade adhesive — no sutures to remove later.

What happens during a Ziplyft procedure?

  1. Your eyelid evaluation

    Dr. Silk evaluates your eyelids, eye health, anatomy, excess skin and goals to decide whether Ziplyft is appropriate.

  2. Marking & numbing

    The excess skin to be treated is carefully marked, and local anesthetic numbs the area.

  3. Compress, remove & seal

    CoCo™ technology compresses the tissue, the excess skin is removed, and the edges are sealed with medical-grade adhesive.

  4. Go home

    There is no operating-room recovery, and no later appointment just to remove stitches.

  5. Heal & see the result

    Swelling and bruising settle as the eyelids heal; most patients are event-ready in about 7–10 days.

Am I a good candidate for Ziplyft?

Ziplyft is designed primarily for adults with excess upper-eyelid skin who want a more open, refreshed appearance and are interested in a minimally invasive alternative to traditional upper blepharoplasty.

You may want to explore Ziplyft if you:

  • Have heavy or hooded upper eyelids
  • Feel your eyes look tired even when you aren’t
  • Have visible excess upper-eyelid skin
  • Want a more open, refreshed appearance
  • Prefer an in-office procedure
  • Want to avoid sutures
  • Want less downtime than traditional surgery may require

Not every heavy eyelid is the same.

True eyelid ptosis, significant excess fat, muscle involvement, previous eyelid surgery and other anatomical conditions can create similar concerns but may need a different or additional treatment.

That’s why the first step should be an examination — not choosing a procedure online.

How long do Ziplyft results last?

Ziplyft physically removes excess upper-eyelid skin rather than temporarily tightening its appearance.

The skin that is removed does not grow back. Your eyelids and surrounding tissues will, however, continue to change naturally as you age — and your anatomy, age, skin quality and lifestyle all influence how.

Is Ziplyft actually an eyelid lift?

Yes. Ziplyft physically removes excess upper-eyelid skin rather than simply tightening or treating the surface of the skin. It is designed as a minimally invasive upper eyelid lift for appropriate patients.

How long does Ziplyft take?

The procedure typically takes less than 15 minutes. Your total appointment will be longer to allow time for evaluation, preparation, marking, local anesthetic and post-treatment instructions.

Does Ziplyft require stitches?

No. Ziplyft uses medical-grade tissue adhesive to close the treatment area instead of sutures, so there are no stitches that need to be removed later.

Are you awake during Ziplyft?

Yes. Ziplyft is performed using local anesthetic and does not require IV sedation or general anesthesia.

Does Ziplyft hurt?

Local anesthetic is used to numb the eyelids before treatment. Individual experiences vary, but the procedure is designed to be performed comfortably in the physician’s office.

How long is Ziplyft recovery?

Most patients are considered event-ready within approximately 7–10 days. Some swelling, redness or bruising is normal while the eyelids heal.

How is Ziplyft different from blepharoplasty?

Both procedures can remove excess upper-eyelid skin. Ziplyft is performed in-office using local anesthetic, without sutures or cautery, and typically takes less than 15 minutes. Traditional upper blepharoplasty uses a more involved surgical approach and typically has a longer recovery.

Is Ziplyft better than blepharoplasty?

Not for every patient. The better procedure depends on what is causing your eyelids to appear heavy and what needs to be corrected. Ziplyft may be ideal for some excess-skin cases, while ptosis, significant fat or muscle involvement and more complex anatomy may require another approach.

Can Ziplyft treat ptosis?

Ziplyft primarily addresses excess upper-eyelid skin. Patients who require true ptosis repair may need a different or additional procedure.

Can Ziplyft remove eyelid fat?

Some patients have concerns beyond excess skin. Significant fat or muscle correction may require an additional incision or another approach. Dr. Silk can determine what is contributing to your eyelid appearance during the examination.

Does Ziplyft leave a scar?

Any procedure that removes skin creates an incision that must heal. The treatment line is placed within the natural upper-eyelid crease and Ziplyft uses adhesive rather than sutures. How the area ultimately heals varies by patient.

Are Ziplyft results permanent?

The excess skin removed during Ziplyft does not grow back. Natural aging continues, however, so your eyelids and surrounding tissues will continue to change over time.

What age is best for Ziplyft?

There isn’t one ideal age. Candidacy depends more on your eyelid anatomy, amount of excess skin, eye health and treatment goals than on a particular age.
